Add a clue

Every team has its own clues at its own locations. Each clue is one QR station: the clue text is the riddle that leads that team to the place where this clue's QR is stuck, and scanning it unlocks their next clue. The single treasure clue is shared: it is every team's last stop, and the first team to scan it wins.
